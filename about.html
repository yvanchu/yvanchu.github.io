Hafa adai!

I'm Yvan (pronounced like "even"). I spent the first 9 years of my life in Taiwan, then 9 more in Guam, where I met my greatest mentor — my robotics club advisor. Her guidance gave me direction: I rallied teams of students to compete in STEM competitions from drone design to underwater robotics, and the long conversations about the future of technology convinced me I could make a difference.

I was fortunate to receive a full ride to Northwestern University as a QuestBridge Scholar. I came in as a manufacturing and design engineering major — it seemed like the perfect major to build things. Then the pandemic hit, the machine shop closed, and I pivoted to computer science. Turns out most startups are software startups anyway, so that worked out.

In college I co-founded BearRoom, led HCI research, interned at Microsoft, and spent a summer at Techstars Chicago learning about product-market fit alongside 12 startup founders. After graduating magna cum laude, I joined Microsoft full-time on the Loop Canvas team (now M365 Copilot Pages), where I've shipped features used by millions — Citations, Copilot Shortcuts, and more.
