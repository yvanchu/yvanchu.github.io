Build technology that improves the way people live, work, and play.

Work

Technology should automate the mundane and empower people to reach and stay in flow. Work can be incredibly meaningful and fulfilling, but it often isn't. I build tools to help eliminate boring, stressful, and confusing work.

That's why I joined Microsoft — to make collaboration seamless for millions of people. And it's why I'm drawn to AI-native tools that give people superpowers instead of replacing them.

Life

I'm most hopeful about technology that saves time and increases safety. But more broadly, I believe the best tools are the ones that give people back their agency — the freedom to spend time on what matters to them.

Play

The digital ecosystem is full of junk content — entertaining but not fulfilling, addicting and mostly driven by engagement metrics. I want to build experiences that are playful, interactive, and genuinely fun. Experiences that help people strengthen existing relationships and build new ones.

That's why I co-founded BearRoom, and why I'm building Bullet Poker now.
